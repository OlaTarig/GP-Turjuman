Place the following MediaPipe model files in this directory:

1. pose_landmarker_full.task (~29 MB)
   Download: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/pose_landmarker/pose_landmarker_full/float16/latest/pose_landmarker_full.task

2. hand_landmarker.task (~8 MB)
   Download: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/hand_landmarker/hand_landmarker/float16/latest/hand_landmarker.task

These are loaded by SignRecognitionChannel.kt via Android's AssetManager (not Flutter's rootBundle).
For smaller APK size, use pose_landmarker_lite.task instead of pose_landmarker_full.task —
but accuracy will be lower and may not match training distribution.
